How each test works →
1 · Statistical Significance
One-sample, one-sided t-test that the mean per-trade P&L is greater than zero. We report p — the chance of seeing this record if the true edge were zero. Pass below 0.05.
2 · Confidence Interval (95%)
The 95% interval around the mean per-trade dollar result. Passes when the lower bound is still above $0 — the edge holds even at the pessimistic end of the interval.
3 · Profit Factor
Gross dollars won ÷ gross dollars lost. Pass above 1.50.
4 · Outlier Independence
Delete the three largest winners and re-total. Passes if the record is still net positive — the edge isn't carried by a few lucky prints.
5 · R-Expectancy
Expected dollars per trade expressed in units of the average loss (1R). Pass above +0.20R.
6 · Breakeven Buffer
How far the actual win rate sits above the win rate a profit factor of 1.0 would demand, in percentage points. Pass above 10 pp.
7 · Streak Resilience
The longest run of consecutive losses in the record. Pass at or below 4.
8 · Bootstrap P(profit)
Resample the trades 2,000 times with replacement (seeded, reproducible); the share of resampled records that finish profitable. Pass above 85%.
